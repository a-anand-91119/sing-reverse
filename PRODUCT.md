# Sing Reverse

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People playing a two-person, pass-the-phone singing game. The requested redesign prioritizes simple instructions, large buttons, and a modern game feel.

## Product Purpose

Record a phrase, listen backwards, imitate the backwards audio, then reverse the imitation to hear how closely it matches.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose; Android 8+. Recordings remain in memory. No accounts, ads, analytics, or internet permission. Preserve recording, normal playback, backwards playback, and microphone permission handling.

## Product Principles

- One obvious next action.
- Explain the current turn in a short sentence.
- Preserve replay and re-record controls without presenting everything at once.

## Confirmed Flow

The user confirmed one turn screen per player, with a large Record button followed by Listen and Pass the phone. No specific palette or typeface was requested.
